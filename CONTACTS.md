# Who to show X-Ray to in London — per use case

**Reality from research:** the fastest "yes today" money is **single operators who can sign on the
spot** (markets, gyms, coworking, exhibitors). **BIDs/councils** are real budget but committee-paced
— treat as pipeline. **Verify any named person on LinkedIn before using their name** (people move
roles; names below are from current public pages, not re-verified employment).

**Universal cold pitch:** *"Free 2-week pilot. I plug in a laptop — no cameras, no install, no
hardware, fully anonymous (GDPR-clean). You get live density, dwell and movement for this space.
If it's not useful, you've lost nothing."*

## 2. ⏱️ QueuePulse — live queue & wait-time
- **Mercato Metropolitano** & **Boxpark** food vendors (visible queues, walk-aways hurt).

- Independent **cafés/clinics**: Exmouth Market, Maltby Street / Spa Terminus, Lower Marsh (Waterloo) — owner decides on the spot.

## 3. 🛟 SafeCount — crowd-density & capacity (Martyn's-Law evidence)
- **Tobacco Dock** (events) · **Old Truman Brewery event halls** · **Coal Drops Yard event team** (Argent, King's Cross) · **Boxpark** event programming.
- Nightlife/large venues (licence capacity pain): **Fabric**, **Drumsheds/Broadwater**, **Troxy**.
- Channel/credibility: **Cross River Partnership** (crossriverpartnership.org) · council **licensing/safety teams**.
- Framing: "audit-ready evidence for your risk assessment," NOT "compliance mandate."

## 4. 📊 BusyNow — public "how busy now" widget

- Bars/cafés wanting an embeddable live badge for web/Google. (Note: monetisation is softer here — Google busyness is free; lead with gyms.)
