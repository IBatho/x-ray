Real-time, anonymous crowd density & movement for any space — from a laptop, with zero extra hardware. No cameras, no apps, no installation. Pick a use case to launch a live dashboard.

📶 WiFi tracking

We read the WiFi access points around you and how many devices are connected to each one (every router reports its associated-device count). That's a live, hardware-free estimate of how many phones/laptops are in range — plus signal-based distance. Great for overall density.

🔵 Bluetooth tracking

We passively scan Bluetooth-LE advertisements from personal devices — earbuds, watches, bands, beacons, some phones. These move with people, so the radar tracks movement and proximity around you in real time. Great for flow, dwell and "who's near."

🔒 Privacy by design: device IDs are salted-hashed and thrown away; the salt rotates so no one can be followed over time. Only aggregate counts leave the box. Modern phones randomize their MAC/BLE address — so this is an honest relative density & trend index, never an exact headcount and never a person tracker.
