# X-Ray — walk-in pitch scripts & demo

**The 10-second line:** *"It's a crowd sensor that runs on a laptop — no cameras, no hardware, no
install, fully anonymous. It doesn't just count people; it tells you what to do."*

**Sense → Insight → Action.** Every screen ends in an instruction, not a chart. That's the whole pitch.

**Honesty line (say it first — it wins trust):** *"Phones randomize their IDs, so this is an honest
relative density & movement index, not exact headcount — and that's exactly why it's GDPR-clean.
We're best at counts, peaks, capture rate and movement; per-person dwell is directional."*

---

## 🏢 FlexFloor — coworking / office (strongest willingness to pay)
**Walk into:** Huckletree, Fora, Uncommon, Second Home, or a corporate workplace/facilities team.
**Say:** *"You pay rent on every square foot. Do you know, live, how much actually gets used? I show
desk and room utilization with no per-desk sensors, no cameras, no badge data — just a laptop."*
**Benefits:** kill the biggest cost leak (empty space); catch **ghost bookings** (rooms booked, sat
empty); right-size cleaning/AC to real use.
**Action it gives:** *"Floor 22% full at 3pm — consolidate or sublet."* / *"Skip cleaning the empty
zones."* / *"Near full — upsell more memberships."*
**Why us:** Density charges $149/sensor + SaaS per sq ft; we're hardware-free, and anonymity beats
camera/badge systems on privacy.

## 🛟 SafeCount — events / venues (most acute pain)
**Walk into:** Tobacco Dock, Coal Drops Yard / Truman Brewery event teams, a club/festival ops lead.
**Say:** *"How do you prove a room never went over capacity? Live density, an alarm before it's
dangerous, and a timestamped log for your safety file. Five-minute setup, no cameras."*
**Benefits:** avoid the reputational/legal nightmare of overcrowding; **audit-ready evidence** for the
risk assessment every 200+ venue now does.
**Action:** door staff get "STOP entry / one-in-one-out"; the log is your compliance artifact.
**Honesty:** Martyn's Law doesn't *mandate* counting — pitch as *"evidence that strengthens your risk
assessment,"* never "compliance you must buy."

## 🎪 StandIQ — exhibitors (easiest paid pilot, per-event)
**Walk into:** a brand's marketing manager at ExCeL; organisers (Clarion, Hyve); via Plug and Play UK.
**Say:** *"You spent £20k on this stand. Can you prove anyone engaged? I show how many stopped and
how long, per zone — so you prove ROI and fix the layout by tomorrow."*
**Action:** *"Visitors linger in the back-left (42s) — move your demo there."* / screenshot for the
post-show ROI report.
**Why it pays:** stands exist to be measured; exhibitors pay willingly per event.

## 🛍️ Shopfront — markets, pop-ups, small independents (the underserved wedge)
**Walk into:** Old Spitalfields, Camden Market (LabTech), Boxpark, Truman Brewery, Mercato; or any
stall/independent owner.
**Don't say "footfall counter."** Everyone has those. **Say:** *"Counters bolt to a wall you don't
own — useless for a stall or pop-up. I plug in a laptop and give you the data you've never had, and
tell you what to do with it."*
**Three action framings:**
- **Staff the right hour:** *"Your busiest 90 minutes today — be ready, don't pay staff to stand idle."* (Labour = 15–25% of a small retailer's sales; matched scheduling cuts 10–20% of hours.)
- **Test your front, keep what sells:** *"Run display A today, B tomorrow — I'll tell you which pulled more people in."* (The stall "window" changes daily and costs nothing to test; A/B winners claim +3–8% entries.)
- **Conversion:** *"200 stopped, 12 bought — here's the one fix."* (Tie counts to your till total, no POS needed.)
**Price wedge:** cheapest incumbent (Dor) = $300 hardware + $150/mo on contract. We're zero hardware,
no contract, and work where they can't install at all.

## ⏱️ QueuePulse — cafés / street food / clinics
**Walk into:** Mercato/Boxpark vendors, KERB markets, independent cafés.
**Say:** *"When your queue gets too long, people walk. I tell you to open a second till *before* that
happens — and you can show customers a live wait time so they stay."*
**Action:** *"~9 min wait — open till 2 now."*

## Business model (say it on a slide)
- **Free 2-week pilot** → **£/month per space, no contract, no hardware** (undercut Dor's $150/mo).
- **Per-event rental** for StandIQ / SafeCount (exhibitions, one-off events).
- **Platform play:** one engine, 10 verticals — land in one, expand across a customer's estate.

## Win-the-room checklist
1. Open with the honesty line (relative index + GDPR-clean). Judges reward it.
2. Demo `combo` mode live — walk a phone/earbuds across the room, watch the radar move.
3. Click into 2 lenses (FlexFloor + SafeCount) — show the **Recommended actions** updating.
4. End with: *"We validated this with [real operator] in London today."* Get one verbal yes first.
